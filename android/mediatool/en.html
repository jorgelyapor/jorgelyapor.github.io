1. Developer and contact

This policy explains how information is handled when you use Media Toolbox for Android, identified by the package com.lynaje.media_toolbox. It describes the current version of the app, which processes files locally and does not include advertising or usage analytics services.

Developer: Lynaje

Privacy contact: jlybest08@hotmail.com

2. Information the app uses

Media Toolbox accesses files you choose through Android's file and media pickers, including videos, images, animations, and images used for watermarks or overlays. These files may contain personal information depending on their content.

To provide its features, the app may process and store on your device:

  • Selected file contents and technical information such as file name, size, duration, dimensions, orientation, format, and audio or video tracks.
  • Access references to original files and editing settings, output names, watermark text and images, and file and page order.
  • History, job status and settings, projects and presets you save, exported results, previews, and temporary files.
  • Interface preferences such as theme and language.

This information supports opening, previewing, editing, and exporting files, recovering interrupted jobs, and reusing settings. You do not need to create an account in the app.

3. Local processing and communications

Editing and conversion take place on your device. The app does not automatically send your files, projects, history, or preferences to the developer's servers. Its current version does not request Internet access permission.

It does not include advertising SDKs, user tracking, advertising identifiers, or services that automatically send analytics or crash reports to the developer. It also does not request contacts, location, camera, or microphone access for its current features.

If you choose files from a cloud provider or decide to save or share results with other apps, those services may transfer data under their own responsibility. This policy distinguishes those actions you request from Media Toolbox's local processing.

4. Storage, retention, and deletion

History results are kept in the app's private storage until you delete them. Projects, presets, and job records are retained locally to preserve your settings and support recovery. Imported watermark images may be stored as normalized copies in the same private space.

  • History: deleting results or clearing history removes finished records and their private outputs. Original files and active jobs are preserved.
  • Projects and presets: you can delete them from their respective sections. Deleting them does not remove original files or separately saved exports.
  • Storage: Free up space removes previews, temporary files, and other unreferenced files; it protects history results and resources still in use.
  • Internal data: you can clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove its private data. This does not delete copies you saved outside the app or shared with other services.

Previews and temporary files may be removed when discarded, through the app's cleanup controls, or when Android manages its cache. Irrecoverable physical erasure of storage is not guaranteed.

Android may include some preferences in backups, depending on your settings and device system. Media Toolbox's configuration excludes history and job settings, private outputs, and watermark images from Android backup and device transfer. External copies you choose to save depend on your selected provider.

5. File access, notifications, and background work

File selection and saving use Android mechanisms. The app may retain read permission for selected references to reopen projects or retry jobs when the provider allows it. You can review available access and permissions in Android settings.

On Android versions that require it, the app requests permission to display progress, completion, and cancellation notifications. Denying this permission does not prevent exports. Notifications may display a file name and job status, including on your lock screen if allowed by your system settings.

Foreground service and screen-off processing permissions are used to run the queue in the background. They are not used to track your activity outside the app.

6. Saving, sharing, and Google Play

Sharing a result gives your selected app temporary read permission for that file. Saving a copy writes the result to the destination you choose. Selected recipients and providers may retain, sync, or use those files under their own policies; Media Toolbox does not control those copies.

The app does not ask for or process card or bank account details to use its tools. If you purchase the app through Google Play, Google handles the purchase and payment information under its own terms.

Android and Google Play may process installation, performance, crash, and purchase information under their own settings and policies. These platform features are separate from services incorporated by Media Toolbox. You can consult Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Information protection

Internal files are stored in the private space Android assigns to the app. Sharing uses the system's temporary read permissions. Access to your information also depends on your device's security, its screen lock, and the apps or destinations you choose to share with.

Android may generate local diagnostic messages when an error occurs. The app does not include a service that automatically sends them to the developer.

8. Questions and requests about your information

You can manage local files and settings using the app and Android controls described in this policy. The developer does not maintain a copy of your media, projects, or history on a server from which it could be recovered or deleted remotely.

If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and any information or files you choose to include. They will be used to address your inquiry and retained for as long as needed for that purpose and to meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion using the privacy contact, subject to those obligations.

For questions about this policy, information handling, or privacy requests, use the contact listed in section 1.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when features or information-handling practices change. The date at the top identifies its latest revision. Changes requiring additional information or consent will be communicated through the appropriate channels.

If a future version adds advertising, analytics, or other services that transmit information, this policy must be updated to explain those services and their privacy options before they become operational. This policy does not describe an AdMob integration because the current version does not include one.
